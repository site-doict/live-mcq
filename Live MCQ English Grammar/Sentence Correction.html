১০. Identify the incorrect word/phrase indicated by (1), (2), (3) or (4) in the following sentence: A doctor may be able "to diagnose" (1) a problem "perfect" (2) but he may not able to find a drug "to which" (3) the patient "will respond" (4). ৩২তম বিসিএস

১১. Identify the incorrect word/phrase indicated by (1), (2), (3) or (4) in the following sentence: According to experts a good way 'to' (1) 'improve' (2) listening skills is 'by' (3) 'watch' (4) television, specially news and documentaries. ৩২তম বিসিএস

১৭. Which of the following sentences is correct? ২৭তম ও ১৬তম বিসিএস
