📌 Shakespeare's Four Great Tragedies

  • Hamlet: Tragedy of indecision and procrastination ("To be or not to be, that is the question").
  • Othello: Tragedy of sexual jealousy manipulated by Iago.
  • King Lear: Tragedy of filial ingratitude and blind flattery.
  • Macbeth: Tragedy of vaulting ambition ("Fair is foul, and foul is fair").
