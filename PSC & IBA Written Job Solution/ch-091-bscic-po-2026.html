মূলভাব: প্রকৃত শিক্ষা কখনো বাইরে থেকে জোরপূর্বক চাপিয়ে দেওয়া যায় না। প্রাতিষ্ঠানিক সনদ বা ডিগ্রি অর্জন করলেই একজন মানুষ প্রকৃত অর্থে শিক্ষিত হতে পারে না। সত্যিকারের জ্ঞানী হতে হলে আত্মপ্রচেষ্টা ও স্বীয় সাধনায় স্বশিক্ষিত হতে হয়।

সম্প্রসারিত ভাব: বিদ্যালয়, মহাবিদ্যালয় ও বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় মানুষকে বিদ্যার সন্ধান দেয় এবং একটি নির্দিষ্ট পাঠ্যক্রমের মধ্য দিয়ে সনদ অর্জনে সহায়তা করে। কিন্তু এ শিক্ষা অনেক সময়ই মুখস্থবিদ্যা ও পরীক্ষায় পাসের সংকীর্ণ গণ্ডিতে সীমাবদ্ধ থাকে। প্রকৃত শিক্ষা মানুষের অন্তরকে আলোকিত করে, সুপ্ত মানবিক সত্তা ও চিন্তাশক্তিকে জাগ্রত করে। এই মানসিক মুক্তি কেবল প্রাতিষ্ঠানিক ডিগ্রির মাধ্যমে লাভ করা সম্ভব নয়; এর জন্য প্রয়োজন গভীর জ্ঞানপিপাসা, আত্মঅনুসন্ধান এবং স্বীয় তাগিদে জ্ঞানের চর্চা।

জগতের মহামানব, বিজ্ঞানী, সাহিত্যিক ও চিন্তাবিদদের দিকে তাকালে দেখা যায়, তাঁদের অধিকাংশই ছিলেন মূলত স্বশিক্ষিত। তাঁরা আনুষ্ঠানিক শিক্ষার চেয়ে নিজ উদ্যোগে প্রকৃতির পাঠশালা ও মানবসমাজ থেকে জ্ঞান আহরণ করেছেন। প্রাতিষ্ঠানিক শিক্ষা সহায়ক হতে পারে, তবে আত্মজ্ঞান ও নৈতিক মূল্যবোধ অর্জন করতে হলে ব্যক্তিকে নিজের অন্তরের আলোতেই উদ্ভাসিত হতে হয়।

মন্তব্য: যে ব্যক্তি নিজের আগ্রহে নিত্যনতুন জ্ঞান অন্বেষণ করে এবং নিজের ভুল শুধরে বিবেকবান মানুষ হিসেবে গড়ে ওঠে, সেই প্রকৃত সুশিক্ষিত। তাই যথার্থ সুশিক্ষা অর্জনের পূর্বশর্ত হলো স্বশিক্ষিত হওয়া।

Democracy in the Modern World

Democracy is widely regarded as one of the most egalitarian and civilized forms of governance in human history. At its philosophical core, it is a government designed of the people, by the people, and for the people. The cornerstone of this system is the fundamental right of ordinary citizens to participate in national decision-making and freely elect their chosen representatives through credible, transparent, and fair elections.

In a healthy democratic dispensation, voters critically evaluate every aspiring candidate based on their vision, integrity, track record, and commitment to the collective welfare of the nation. An ideal political leader does not perceive state authority as a personal privilege; rather, a true leader acts as a humble servant of the public, faithfully upholding the rule of law, institutional transparency, and individual fundamental rights. Conversely, when absolute power becomes centralized in the hands of a single authoritarian individual or an entrenched oligarchy without judicial checks and balances, the state inevitably descends into ruthless tyranny and systemic oppression.

To thwart autocratic tendencies and preserve civic liberty, citizens must remain consistently informed about governance affairs, socio-economic policies, and legislative developments. An enlightened, vigilant, and politically informed electorate forms the ultimate bulwark against tyranny. Therefore, democracy is not merely a cyclical voting ritual; it is a living civic culture that safeguards human dignity, fosters pluralism, and ensures social progress for all.

Give your statement on the following sentence and argue using examples, facts, and your own experience:

💡 সমাধান ও প্রামাণ্য বিশ্লেষণ দেখুন ▼

Argumentative Essay: Digital Devices and Social Interaction

Statement: The assertion that \"Excessive use of cell phones and digital devices is the main reason for reduced social interaction\" highlights one of the most visible behavioral shifts of the 21st century. While digital technology is undeniably the primary visible catalyst for decreasing face-to-face engagements, attributing social fragmentation solely to smartphones oversimplifies broader socio-economic and structural transformations in modern living.

Arguments for the Proposition (The Illusion of Connection):

  1. Erosion of Real-time Presence ('Phubbing'): The omnipresence of smartphones has normalized 'phubbing' (phone-snubbing)—ignoring real-world companions to gaze at a screen. Family dinners, cafe conversations, and social gatherings are now plagued by divided attention.
  2. Loss of Spontaneous Micro-interactions: Historically, waiting at bus stops, train stations, or elevator lobbies encouraged informal chit-chat with strangers. Today, headphones and touchscreens serve as anti-social shields, severing spontaneous human connections.
  3. Dopamine-driven Isolation: Algorithms engineered by tech corporations are intentionally designed to exploit human psychology through infinite scrolling, replacing fulfilling interpersonal intimacy with shallow digital validation (likes and shares).

Counter-arguments & Broader Reality:

However, digital devices often fill a void created by modern urban design, demanding corporate work hours, hyper-individualism, and the vanishing of community parks and cultural centers ('third places'). Furthermore, digital platforms enable diasporic families, remote workers, and niche communities across continents to stay intimately connected when physical proximity is impossible.

Conclusion: In conclusion, excessive cell phone use acts as an accelerant rather than the singular root cause of declining interpersonal communion. The sustainable remedy is not demonizing technology, but practicing \"digital intentionality\"—establishing screen-free boundaries and consciously prioritizing genuine human touch and conversation.

প্রশ্ন নং ৬ গণিত গণিত পূর্ণমান: ১৫
Mathematics:
a) Money is invested for 6, 8 and 10 years at simple interest rates of 4%, 8% and 14% respectively. If the simple interest earned from all three investments is equal, find the ratio of the three investments.
b) A rectangular park has an area of 1,380 sq. ft. Only 3 sides need to be fenced; one side measures 30 ft. How many feet of fencing are needed?
c) Pipes A and B can fill a tank in 60 minutes and 40 minutes respectively. If the tank is filled from empty by Pipe B alone for half the total time and by A + B together for the other half, how long does it take to fill the tank?
💡 সমাধান ও প্রামাণ্য বিশ্লেষণ দেখুন ▼

(a) Simple Interest Ratio Problem:

Let the three principal amounts invested be \(x, y,\) and \(z\) respectively.

Given simple interest \(SI = \frac{P \times r \times t}{100}\):

  • \(SI_1 = \frac{x \times 4 \times 6}{100} = \frac{24x}{100}\)
  • \(SI_2 = \frac{y \times 8 \times 8}{100} = \frac{64y}{100}\)
  • \(SI_3 = \frac{z \times 14 \times 10}{100} = \frac{140z}{100}\)

Since the interests are equal:
\(\frac{24x}{100} = \frac{64y}{100} = \frac{140z}{100} \Rightarrow 24x = 64y = 140z\)

Dividing by 4:
\(6x = 16y = 35z = k\)
\(\Rightarrow x = \frac{k}{6}, y = \frac{k}{16}, z = \frac{k}{35}\)

The L.C.M. of 6, 16, and 35 is \(1680\) (or using 24, 64, 140 with LCM = 6,720):
Ratio \(x : y : z = \frac{1}{24} : \frac{1}{64} : \frac{1}{140}\)
Multiplying by 6,720:
\(x : y : z = \frac{6720}{24} : \frac{6720}{64} : \frac{6720}{140} = 280 : 105 : 48\).

Answer: 280 : 105 : 48.

(b) Rectangular Park Fencing:

Area of the rectangle \(A = \text{length} \times \text{breadth} = 1380\text{ sq. ft.}\)

Given one side is \(30\text{ ft}\). Let this side be breadth \(b = 30\text{ ft}\).
Then length \(l = \frac{1380}{30} = 46\text{ ft}\).

Only 3 sides need to be fenced (one length and two breadths, or one breadth and two lengths).
If the unfenced side is one length (\(46\text{ ft}\)):
Fencing required \(= l + 2b = 46 + 2(30) = 46 + 60 = 106\text{ ft}\).
If the unfenced side is one breadth (\(30\text{ ft}\)):
Fencing required \(= b + 2l = 30 + 2(46) = 30 + 92 = 122\text{ ft}\).

Answer: 122 feet (or 106 feet depending on which side is unfenced; standard exam solution: 122 feet).

(c) Pipes and Cistern Problem:

Pipe A fills the tank in 60 minutes \(\Rightarrow\) Rate of A \(= \frac{1}{60}\) per minute.
Pipe B fills the tank in 40 minutes \(\Rightarrow\) Rate of B \(= \frac{1}{40}\) per minute.
Combined rate of (A + B) \(= \frac{1}{60} + \frac{1}{40} = \frac{2+3}{120} = \frac{5}{120} = \frac{1}{24}\) per minute.

Let the total time taken to fill the tank be \(T\) minutes.
Pipe B operates alone for \(\frac{T}{2}\) minutes, and Pipes (A + B) operate together for \(\frac{T}{2}\) minutes.

Equation of total work done:
\(\left(\frac{T}{2} \times \frac{1}{40}\right) + \left(\frac{T}{2} \times \frac{1}{24}\right) = 1\)
\(\Rightarrow \frac{T}{80} + \frac{T}{48} = 1\)
\(\Rightarrow T \left(\frac{3 + 5}{240}\right) = 1\)
\(\Rightarrow T \left(\frac{8}{240}\right) = 1\)
\(\Rightarrow \frac{T}{30} = 1 \Rightarrow T = 30\text{ minutes}\).

Answer: 30 minutes.
