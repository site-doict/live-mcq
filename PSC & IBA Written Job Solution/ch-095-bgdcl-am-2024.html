বাখরাবাদ গ্যাস অর্থায়ন পরীক্ষার ক্যাশ ফ্লো বিবরণী ও অভ্যন্তরীণ নিরীক্ষা নীতিমালার গুরুত্বপূর্ণ সারসংক্ষেপ:

📊 ক্যাশ ফ্লো বিবরণী (Indirect Method)

  • Operating Activities: Net Income + Non-cash expenses (Depreciation) + চলতি সম্পদ হ্রাস / চলতি দায় বৃদ্ধি – চলতি সম্পদ বৃদ্ধি / চলতি দায় হ্রাস।
  • Investing Activities: স্থায়ী সম্পদ ও জমি বিক্রয় (+) / নতুন সরঞ্জাম ক্রয় (–)।
  • Financing Activities: শেয়ার ইস্যু (+) / লভ্যাংশ প্রদান (–) / বন্ড পরিশোধ (–)।

🔒 Principles of Internal Control

  • Separation of Duties: হিসাবরক্ষণ, অনুমোদন ও সম্পদ হেফাজতের দায়িত্ব আলাদা রাখা।
  • Independent Verification: আকস্মিক অডিট, ব্যাঙ্ক রিকনসিলিয়েশন ও ট্রায়াল ব্যালেন্স যাচাই।
  • Documentation: ক্রমিক নম্বরযুক্ত ইনভয়েস ও ভাউচার ব্যবহার বাধ্যতামূলক করা।

What is internal control? Discuss the principles of internal control activities.
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Definition of Internal Control:

Internal Control is a comprehensive process designed, implemented, and maintained by those charged with governance, management, and other personnel to provide reasonable assurance regarding the achievement of an organization's objectives concerning:

  1. Reliability of financial reporting;
  2. Effectiveness and efficiency of operations; and
  3. Compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Internal management controls safeguard corporate assets against fraud, theft, and unauthorized acquisition or disposition, while ensuring accounting records are accurate and dependable.

Key Principles of Internal Control Activities:

  1. Separation of Duties (Segregation of Functions): Operational duties must be compartmentalized so that no single employee controls all phases of a transaction (e.g., authorization, custody of assets, and record-keeping must remain strictly separate). Bookkeepers should not handle cash deposits, and check signers should not approve vendor invoices.
  2. Establishment of Responsibility: Clear organizational assignments must ensure that specific individuals are solely responsible for specific tasks (e.g., dedicated cash register access codes).
  3. Physical and Digital Access Controls: Protecting physical assets (fireproof vaults, electronic keycards, surveillance) and digital ledgers (two-factor authentication, encrypted databases, user permission tiers) to prevent unauthorized entry.
  4. Documentation Procedures: Standardizing pre-numbered paper and digital invoices, receipts, and purchase orders ensures every business event leaves a traceable audit trail and eliminates ghost transactions.
  5. Independent Internal Verification (Physical Audits): Periodic unannounced audits, bank reconciliations, inventory counts, and surprise asset inspections by internal audit teams independent of daily operations.
  6. Trial Balance and Double-Entry Verification: Systematically balancing general ledger accounts to verify arithmetical accuracy and detect reporting discrepancies early.
  7. Human Resource Controls (Bonding & Mandatory Vacations): Conducting thorough background checks on key financial personnel, obtaining fidelity bonds, and rotating key duties with mandatory leave to uncover concealed irregularities.
