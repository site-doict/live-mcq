Essay: Non-Performing Loans in Bangladesh

Introduction: Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) have become one of the major challenges facing the banking sector of Bangladesh. In general, a loan becomes non-performing when the borrower fails to repay principal or interest according to the prescribed schedule and the loan meets the regulatory criteria for classification. A high level of NPLs weakens banks, restricts the flow of credit, discourages investment and ultimately affects economic growth. Therefore, reducing NPLs is essential for maintaining financial stability and ensuring sustainable economic development in Bangladesh.

Causes of Non-Performing Loans: There are several reasons behind the accumulation of NPLs in Bangladesh. Weak credit assessment and inadequate monitoring of borrowers are among the major causes. In some cases, loans are sanctioned without properly evaluating the borrower's financial capacity, business prospects and repayment ability. Weak corporate governance, irregularities in lending, misuse of borrowed funds and inadequate internal control also contribute to loan defaults. Economic shocks, inflation, rising production costs, exchange-rate pressure and natural disasters can also make it difficult for genuine businesses to repay their loans. Moreover, lengthy legal procedures and difficulties in recovering collateral often delay loan recovery.

Willful Default and Poor Governance: Willful default is another serious concern. Some borrowers may have the financial capacity to repay their loans but deliberately avoid repayment or divert borrowed funds to purposes other than those for which the loans were sanctioned. Repeated rescheduling without sufficient justification can also weaken repayment discipline. Political or influential interference in lending decisions, weak accountability and poor governance in financial institutions can further aggravate the problem. Such practices create an unhealthy credit culture and place responsible borrowers at a disadvantage.

Effects on the Banking Sector and Economy: High NPLs have far-reaching consequences for both banks and the national economy. Banks have to maintain provisions against classified loans, which reduces their profitability and limits their ability to provide new credit. A shortage of available credit may increase borrowing costs and discourage private investment. Small and medium-sized enterprises may suffer particularly when banks become reluctant to provide new loans. As investment declines, employment generation and industrial expansion may also slow down. Persistent NPLs can weaken public confidence in banks and threaten overall financial stability. Furthermore, when financially weak banks require recapitalization or other forms of public support, pressure may ultimately fall on public finances.

Measures to Reduce NPLs: A comprehensive strategy is necessary to address the NPL problem. First, banks must strengthen credit assessment and risk management before sanctioning loans. Borrowers' financial records, repayment capacity, business prospects and credit history should be carefully examined. Second, effective corporate governance and accountability must be ensured in banks. Lending decisions should be based on commercial considerations rather than undue influence. Bangladesh Bank should strengthen supervision and ensure consistent enforcement of banking regulations. Third, the loan recovery process should be made faster and more effective. Legal procedures relating to defaulted loans need to be improved so that cases do not remain unresolved for years. A strong and comprehensive credit information system can also help banks identify borrowers with excessive debt or poor repayment records. Fourth, effective measures should be taken against willful defaulters in accordance with the law.

Way Forward and Conclusion: Bangladesh needs to move from a culture of repeated loan rescheduling towards a culture of responsible lending and repayment. Banks should adopt technology-based credit monitoring, strengthen internal audits and improve the professional capacity of their officials. In conclusion, non-performing loans are not merely a problem of individual banks; they are a threat to the stability and efficiency of the entire financial system. A healthy banking sector based on transparency, accountability and financial discipline is essential for investment, employment generation and sustainable economic growth.

প্রবন্ধ: কৃত্রিম বুদ্ধিমত্তা ও চাকুরির বাজার

ভূমিকা: কৃত্রিম বুদ্ধিমত্তা বা AI আসলে আমাদের কাজ করার ধরনটাকেই পাল্টে দিচ্ছে। আগে যে কাজগুলো করতে অনেক সময় লাগত, এখন সেগুলো মুহূর্তে করা যায়। প্রযুক্তির এই উন্নতি চাকরির বাজারকে যেমন চাঙ্গা করছে, তেমনই নতুন ধরনের চ্যালেঞ্জও ছুড়ে দিচ্ছে। কাজ হারানোর ভয় যেমন আছে, তেমনি নতুন কাজের সুযোগও তৈরি হচ্ছে। এই পরিবর্তনটা শুধু উন্নত দেশেই নয়, বাংলাদেশেও স্পষ্ট। কর্মসংস্থানের কাঠামো তাই নতুনভাবে ভাবতে হচ্ছে।

স্বয়ংক্রিয়তা এবং রুটিন কাজের সংকট: AI সবচেয়ে আগে প্রভাব ফেলেছে রুটিন, পুনরাবৃত্তিমূলক কাজগুলোতে। ডেটা এন্ট্রি, বেসিক হিসাব, প্রাথমিক কাস্টমার সার্ভিস এসব জায়গায় মেশিন এখন মানুষের তুলনায় দ্রুত ও নির্ভুলভাবে কাজ করতে পারে। ফলে এসব কাজের চাহিদা কমছে। অনেক প্রতিষ্ঠান খরচ বাঁচাতে AI-চালিত সফটওয়্যার ব্যবহার করছে। এর অর্থ যে চাকরিগুলো আগে নিরাপদ মনে হতো, এখন সেগুলো ঝুঁকিতে। তবে এটাকে পুরোপুরি নেতিবাচকও বলা যায় না, কারণ মানুষ এখন সময় পাচ্ছে আরও জটিল কাজ শেখার। তবু বাস্তবতা হলো—রুটিন চাকরিতে একটা বড় পরিবর্তন ইতিমধ্যেই ঘটেছে।

নতুন পেশার উত্থান ও চাহিদা বৃদ্ধি: AI শুধু চাকরি কমাচ্ছে না—নতুন চাকরির যাত্রাও শুরু করেছে। ডেটা সায়েন্স, মেশিন লার্নিং, প্রম্পট ইঞ্জিনিয়ারিং, সাইবার সিকিউরিটি, রোবোটিক্স মেইনটেন্যান্স এসব ক্ষেত্র আগে এত জনপ্রিয় ছিল না। এখন এসব পেশায় প্রশিক্ষিত লোকের ঘাটতি দেখা যাচ্ছে। বাংলাদেশেও অনেক আইটি কোম্পানি দক্ষ জনবল খুঁজে পাচ্ছে না। এই পরিবর্তন চাকরির নতুন দিগন্ত খুলে দিচ্ছে। ফলে যারা নতুন প্রযুক্তি শেখার ইচ্ছা রাখে, তাদের জন্য সুযোগ বাড়ছে। AI তাই সংকটের পাশাপাশি সম্ভাবনার দরজাও খুলছে।

দক্ষতার রূপান্তর, শেখার চাপ ও সুযোগ: আজকের চাকরির বাজারে শুধু ডিগ্রি নয়, স্কিলই মূল চালিকা শক্তি। বিশ্লেষণী ক্ষমতা, সমস্যা সমাধানের দক্ষতা, কম্পিউটার জ্ঞান, ডেটা বোঝার ক্ষমতা এসবই এখন প্রয়োজনীয়। AI এর যুগে টিকে থাকতে হলে কর্মীদের নিয়মিত আপডেট থাকতে হবে। অনেকেই হয়তো ভাবতে পারে, প্রযুক্তি শেখা কঠিন। কিন্তু সত্যি হলো যদি ধাপে ধাপে শেখা যায়, তাহলে এটি নতুন সুযোগ তৈরি করে। এই পরিবর্তন আমাদের বাধ্য করছে নিজের দক্ষতা ঝালিয়ে নিতে, আর এটিই ভবিষ্যতের জন্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ বিনিয়োগ।

মানুষের দক্ষতার জায়গা এখনো আলাদা: AI দ্রুত, নির্ভুল—কিন্তু মানুষের মতো অনুভূতি, সৃজনশীলতা, মানবিক বিচারক্ষমতা এখনো তার নেই। শিক্ষকতা, ডাক্তারি, মনোবিজ্ঞান, সাংবাদিকতা, ব্যবস্থাপনা এসব ক্ষেত্রে মানুষের উপস্থিতি অপরিহার্য। কারণ এসব পেশায় যোগাযোগ, সহানুভূতি ও বাস্তব পরিস্থিতির সূক্ষ্ম বিশ্লেষণ খুব প্রয়োজন। তাই ভয় পাওয়ার কিছু নেই, মানুষের জায়গা পুরোপুরি কখনোই হারাবে না। বরং মানুষ আর AI মিলেই ভবিষ্যতের কর্মজগৎ তৈরি করবে। যৌথভাবে কাজ করাটাই পরবর্তী ধাপ।

চ্যালেঞ্জ, অসমতা ও ভবিষ্যতের পথ: AI নির্ভর চাকরির বাজার নতুন এক ধরনের বিভাজন তৈরি করছে। যাদের প্রযুক্তিগত দক্ষতা আছে তারা এগিয়ে যাচ্ছে, অন্যদিকে যারা শেখার সুযোগ পাচ্ছে না তারা পিছিয়ে পড়ছে। এতে আয় বৈষম্য বাড়ার ঝুঁকি তৈরি হয়। অনেক চাকরি অটোমেশনে চলে গেলে কিছু মানুষ অস্থায়ীভাবে কর্মহীন হতে পারে। তাই এই রূপান্তর সামলাতে সরকার, প্রতিষ্ঠান ও শিক্ষা ব্যবস্থাকে এগিয়ে আসতে হবে। সবাই যেন প্রযুক্তিগত সুবিধা পায় এটাই এখন সবচেয়ে বড় চ্যালেঞ্জ।

উপসংহার: সবশেষে একটাই কথা—AI কোনো শত্রু নয়, আবার অন্ধভাবে বিশ্বাস করার জাদুও নয়। এটা একটা শক্তিশালী টুল, যাকে যারা ব্যবহার করতে শিখবে তারাই এগিয়ে যাবে। পরিবর্তনের এই সময়ে ভয় পেলে হবে না; শিখতে হবে, মানিয়ে নিতে হবে, সুযোগগুলো ধরতে হবে। চাকরির বাজার বদলাচ্ছে, আমাদেরও বদলাতে হবে। ভবিষ্যৎ তাদেরই, যারা প্রস্তুত হয়ে সামনে হাঁটতে জানে।

Solve the following mathematical problems:

a) Permutation & Combination: Serial numbers for a product are to be made using 2 letters followed by 3 numbers. If the letters are to be taken from first 8 letters of the alphabet with no repetitions and the numbers are to be taken from the 10 digits (0 - 9) with no repetitions, how many serial numbers are possible?

b) Trigonometry: On the same side of a tower two objects are located. The angles of depression of the two objects observed from the top of the tower are 30° and 60°. If the height of the tower is 150√3 m, find the distance between the two objects.

c) Series: If √2/3 and 8√2/81 be the 4th and 10th terms, respectively, of a geometric series. Find the 7th term of that series.

d) Number Concept: Find a number nearest to 43583, which is divisible by each of 25, 50 and 75.

e) Interest: A debt of Tk. 1,000 with interest at 16% compounded quarterly, is to be amortized by 20 quarterly equal payments over the next 5 years. What will be the amount of these payments?
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Mathematical Solutions:

a) Permutation & Combination:

There are 8 letters and 10 digits (0 to 9). No repetition is allowed.
Ways to choose and arrange 2 letters from 8 letters:
\[ ^8P_2 = \frac{8!}{(8-2)!} = \frac{8!}{6!} = 8 \times 7 = 56 \]
Ways to choose and arrange 3 digits from 10 digits:
\[ ^{10}P_3 = \frac{10!}{(10-3)!} = \frac{10!}{7!} = 10 \times 9 \times 8 = 720 \]
Total possible serial numbers = \( 56 \times 720 = 40,320 \).
Answer: 40,320.

b) Trigonometry:

Let the height of the tower be \( h = 150\sqrt{3} \text{ m} \).
Let the distance of the nearer object from the base of the tower (at angle 60°) be \( x \) meters, and the distance of the farther object (at angle 30°) be \( y \) meters.
From the right triangle for the nearer object:
\[ \tan 60^\circ = \frac{h}{x} \implies \sqrt{3} = \frac{150\sqrt{3}}{x} \implies x = 150 \text{ m} \]
From the right triangle for the farther object:
\[ \tan 30^\circ = \frac{h}{y} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{150\sqrt{3}}{y} \implies y = 150\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3} = 450 \text{ m} \]
Therefore, the distance between the two objects = \( y - x = 450 - 150 = 300 \text{ m} \).
Answer: 300 meters.

c) Geometric Series:

Let the first term be \( a \) and common ratio be \( r \).
\( n \)-th term of a geometric series is \( T_n = a r^{n-1} \).
Given:
\[ T_4 = a r^3 = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} \quad \text{--- (i)} \]
\[ T_{10} = a r^9 = \frac{8\sqrt{2}}{81} \quad \text{--- (ii)} \]
Dividing (ii) by (i):
\[ \frac{a r^9}{a r^3} = r^6 = \frac{8\sqrt{2}/81}{\sqrt{2}/3} = \frac{8\sqrt{2}}{81} \times \frac{3}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{8}{27} \]
\[ (r^2)^3 = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3 \implies r^2 = \frac{2}{3} \implies r = \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}} \]
From (i):
\[ a \times \left(\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\right)^3 = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} \implies a \times \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} \implies a = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \]
Now, the 7th term \( T_7 = a r^6 \):
\[ T_7 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \frac{8}{27} = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{27} \]
Answer: \( \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{27} \).

d) Number Concept:

The numbers are 25, 50, and 75.
LCM(25, 50, 75):
\( 25 = 5^2 \), \( 50 = 2 \times 5^2 \), \( 75 = 3 \times 5^2 \)
\( \text{LCM} = 2 \times 3 \times 25 = 150 \).
Now divide 43,583 by 150:
\( 43,583 \div 150 = 290.553 \)
Lower multiple = \( 290 \times 150 = 43,500 \). Distance = \( 43,583 - 43,500 = 83 \).
Upper multiple = \( 291 \times 150 = 43,650 \). Distance = \( 43,650 - 43,583 = 67 \).
Since 67 < 83, the nearest multiple is 43,650.
Answer: 43,650.

e) Amortization of Loan:

Principal \( p = 1,000 \) Tk.
Annual interest rate = 16% compounded quarterly, so quarterly rate \( r = \frac{16\%}{4} = 4\% = 0.04 \).
Number of quarterly payments \( n = 5 \times 4 = 20 \).
Amortization formula for equal periodic payment \( A \):
\[ A = p \times \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1} \]
Here, \( (1+0.04)^{20} = (1.04)^{20} \approx 2.1911 \).
\[ A = 1,000 \times \frac{0.04 \times 2.1911}{2.1911 - 1} = 1,000 \times \frac{0.08764}{1.1911} \approx 73.583 \text{ Tk.} \]
Answer: Tk. 73.583.

Argumentative Essay: Foreign Aid and Investment Bring More Risk than Benefit for Developing Countries

Introduction: Foreign aid and investment have long been seen as crucial mechanisms for promoting development in poorer nations. Developed countries often provide financial support and investment to help developing countries improve their infrastructure, healthcare, education, and overall economy. However, despite the perceived benefits, the effectiveness of foreign aid and investment has come under scrutiny in recent years. Critics argue that foreign aid and investment often bring more risks than rewards, such as dependency, loss of sovereignty, and the exacerbation of social inequalities. This essay will explore both sides of the argument and conclude with why foreign aid and investment may, in fact, be more of a risk than a benefit for developing countries.

In Favor of the Argument (Risks): The argument against foreign aid and investment highlights several critical issues that can overshadow the perceived benefits. One significant concern is dependency. Developing nations, particularly those in Africa and Asia, often become reliant on continuous aid rather than developing sustainable local industries. When countries are dependent on foreign aid, they lack the incentive to build their own capacities, which can hinder long-term growth and self-sufficiency. Moreover, foreign aid often creates a vicious cycle of debt. Many developing countries accept loans with the promise of economic growth. However, when these loans come with high interest rates or unrealistic repayment schedules, countries fall into a cycle of borrowing more to pay off old debts. This can severely restrict a nation's financial independence and limit their ability to invest in other critical sectors such as education, healthcare, and infrastructure.

Against the Argument (Benefits): Despite the risks mentioned above, foreign aid and investment can bring substantial benefits to developing countries. One of the most notable advantages is the improvement of infrastructure. Many developing countries face severe infrastructural deficits in sectors like transportation, electricity, water, and healthcare. Foreign aid and investment can help build these essential infrastructures, thus stimulating economic growth and improving the quality of life for the local population. Foreign investment can also bring technology transfer and innovation. Many developing countries lack access to the latest technologies and expertise, which can limit their industrial capabilities. By attracting foreign companies and investors, these countries can gain access to advanced technology, knowledge, and management practices, leading to increased productivity and innovation in key sectors such as agriculture, manufacturing, and energy.

Conclusion: In conclusion, while foreign aid and investment can contribute to development, they also pose significant risks to developing countries. The dependency on aid, the accumulation of debt, and the loss of sovereignty are real dangers that cannot be ignored. However, foreign aid and investment also provide critical infrastructure, technology transfer, and emergency support that can help developing countries grow and overcome immediate challenges. Ultimately, the success of foreign aid and investment hinges on how it is managed. If developing countries can strategically navigate the risks and utilize aid and investment to build long-term, sustainable growth, these tools can be beneficial. Without careful management, however, the risks may outweigh the rewards. Thus, it is crucial that developing nations take proactive steps to ensure that foreign aid and investment are used effectively to promote their long-term economic and social well-being.
