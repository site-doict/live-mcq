Some people believe that international sporting events are the ideal opportunity to show the world the qualities of the hosting nation. Others believe that these events are mainly a large unjustifiable expense. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Use your ideas, knowledge and experience and support your opinion with examples and relevant evidences. You should write about 150 words.
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Essay: The Impact of Hosting International Sporting Events

Sports have always been one of the best sources of entertainment and cultural unification for people around the world. When a country hosts an international sporting event—such as the FIFA World Cup or the Olympic Games—despite the massive expenditure involved, it significantly augments tourism, global brand recognition, cultural understanding, and the national economy. I strongly agree with the argument that hosting these mega sporting events yields immense long-term benefits that outweigh the immediate costs.

To begin with, when a country hosts international sporting events, it attracts millions of visitors from across the globe. This gives an immediate boost to the aviation, hospitality, and local retail sectors. For instance, when Russia hosted the 2018 FIFA World Cup, tourists from diverse nations witnessed modern Russian infrastructure, overcoming historical media stereotypes and generating substantial foreign currency inflows. Furthermore, modern stadiums, metro rail lines, and airports constructed for the tournament remain permanent capital assets for future generations. In conclusion, while capital expenditure is high, the resulting infrastructure modernization, international goodwill, and economic vitality make hosting prestigious international sporting events a worthwhile national endeavor.

A shopkeeper buys 100 mangoes at Tk. 12 each. He sells 60 mangoes at Tk. 17.40 each and x mangoes at Tk. 11.31 each. The shopkeeper makes a profit of at least 10%. Find the least possible value of x.
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Solution:

Total cost price of 100 mangoes = \( 100 \times 12 = \text{Tk. } 1,200 \).
The shopkeeper makes a profit of at least 10% on his total investment.
Minimum required selling price = \( 1,200 + (1,200 \times 10\%) = 1,200 + 120 = \text{Tk. } 1,320 \).
Selling price of 60 mangoes = \( 60 \times 17.40 = \text{Tk. } 1,044 \).
Selling price of \( x \) mangoes = \( 11.31x \) Tk.
Total selling price = \( 1,044 + 11.31x \).
According to the condition:
\[ 1,044 + 11.31x \ge 1,320 \]
\[ 11.31x \ge 1,320 - 1,044 \]
\[ 11.31x \ge 276 \]
\[ x \ge \frac{276}{11.31} \approx 24.403 \]
Since the number of mangoes \( x \) must be an integer (a whole mango), the least possible integer value of \( x \) that satisfies \( x \ge 24.403 \) is 25.
Answer: 25 mangoes.

প্রশ্ন নং ৩ গণিত গণিত পূর্ণমান: ১৫
A train has length of 150 meters. It is passing a man who is moving at 2 km/hr in the same direction of the train, in 3 seconds. Find out the speed of the train.
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Solution:

Length of the train \( d = 150 \) meters.
Time taken to pass the man \( t = 3 \) seconds.
Speed of the train relative to the running man:
\[ v_{\text{rel}} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{150 \text{ m}}{3 \text{ s}} = 50 \text{ m/s} \]
Converting relative speed into km/hr:
\[ v_{\text{rel}} = 50 \times \frac{18}{5} = 180 \text{ km/hr} \]
Since the man is moving in the same direction as the train at \( 2 \text{ km/hr} \):
\[ v_{\text{rel}} = v_{\text{train}} - v_{\text{man}} \]
\[ 180 = v_{\text{train}} - 2 \]
\[ v_{\text{train}} = 180 + 2 = 182 \text{ km/hr} \]
Answer: 182 km/hr.

প্রশ্ন নং ৪ গণিত গণিত পূর্ণমান: ১৫
If the height of a right square pyramid is increased by 12%, by what percent must the side of the base be increased, so that the volume of the pyramid is increased by 28%?
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Solution:

Let the original side of the square base be \( x_1 \) and original height be \( h_1 \).
Volume of a right square pyramid \( V = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height} = \frac{1}{3} x^2 h \).
Original volume \( V_1 = \frac{1}{3} x_1^2 h_1 \).
Let the new base side be \( x_2 \) and new height be \( h_2 \).
Given:
New height \( h_2 = h_1 \times (1 + 0.12) = 1.12 h_1 \)
New volume \( V_2 = V_1 \times (1 + 0.28) = 1.28 V_1 \)
Ratio of volumes:
\[ \frac{V_2}{V_1} = \frac{\frac{1}{3} x_2^2 h_2}{\frac{1}{3} x_1^2 h_1} = \left(\frac{x_2}{x_1}\right)^2 \times \left(\frac{h_2}{h_1}\right) \]
\[ 1.28 = \left(\frac{x_2}{x_1}\right)^2 \times 1.12 \]
\[ \left(\frac{x_2}{x_1}\right)^2 = \frac{1.28}{1.12} = \frac{128}{112} = \frac{8}{7} \approx 1.142857 \]
\[ \frac{x_2}{x_1} = \sqrt{\frac{8}{7}} = \sqrt{1.142857} \approx 1.06904 \approx 1.07 \]
Percentage increase in base side:
\[ \text{Percentage Increase} = \left(\frac{x_2}{x_1} - 1\right) \times 100\% = (1.07 - 1) \times 100\% = 7\% \]
Answer: 7%.
