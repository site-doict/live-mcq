📌 বাংলা ব্যাকরণ ও প্রমিত প্রয়োগ

  • সন্ধি সূত্র: যদ্যপি = যদি + অপি; বৃহস্পতি = বৃহৎ + পতি (নিপাতনে সিদ্ধ ব্যঞ্জন); প্রৌঢ় = প্র + ঊঢ় (নিপাতনে সিদ্ধ স্বরসন্ধি)।
  • বাক্য শুদ্ধিকরণ: 'অশ্রুজল' ভুল → 'অশ্রু' শুদ্ধ; 'গৃহস্থের খেয়ে' নয় → 'ঘরের খেয়ে বনের মোষ তাড়ানো'; 'দৈন্যতা' নয় → 'দীনতা' বা 'দৈন্য'।
  • এক কথায় প্রকাশ: বেঁচে থাকার ইচ্ছা = জিজীবিষা; যা প্রমাণ করা যায় না = অপ্রমেয়; ক্ষতিপূরণের অর্থ = খেসারত।
  • সাংস্কৃতিক আন্দোলন: সংস্কৃতি ছিল ১৯৫২ ও ১৯৭১ সালের মুক্তি সংগ্রামের মূল সঞ্জীবনী শক্তি।

🇬🇧 Appropriate Preposition Rules

  • By + Deadline: Received by 24th January (নির্দিষ্ট তারিখের মধ্যে)।
  • Comply with: He complied with my request (সম্মত হওয়া)।
  • Dispose of: He disposed of his house (বিক্রি বা নিস্পত্তি করা)।
  • Count on: Always counts on my help (নির্ভর করা)।

ডুপন্ট অনুপাত বিশ্লেষণ:
• ROA = Net Income / Total Assets = ২০% ⇒ Net Income = ১.৬ মিলিয়ন।
• Net Profit Margin = ১০% ⇒ Sales = ১৬ মিলিয়ন ⇒ TAT = Sales / Assets = ২ বার।

07. A, B and C started a business jointly with a total amount of Tk. 28,000, A paid Tk. 4,500 more than B and B paid Tk. 7,000 less than C. If the company made profit of Tk. 5,600 and profit sharing is in proportion to investment, how much profit should C receive?

স্বমূল্যায়ন:

Solution:

Let C's investment = Tk. \(x\).

∴ B's investment = Tk. \((x - 7,000)\)

∴ A's investment = Tk. \((x - 7,000 + 4,500) =\) Tk. \((x - 2,500)\)

According to the question, total investment:
\(A + B + C = 28,000\)

⇒ \((x - 2,500) + (x - 7,000) + x = 28,000\)

⇒ \(3x - 9,500 = 28,000\)

⇒ \(3x = 37,500\)

⇒ \(x = \frac{37,500}{3} = 12,500\)

So, C's investment is Tk. 12,500.

Total profit = Tk. 5,600.

Since profit is shared in proportion to investment:

Profit received by C = \(5,600 \times \frac{12,500}{28,000} = 5,600 \times \frac{25}{56} = 100 \times 25 = \mathbf{Tk.\ 2,500}\)।

Answer: Tk. 2,500

08. A product is made with three components A, B and C. The ratio of the prices are 4 : 3 : 2. After 1 year, price of A increased by 10%, B increased by 8% and C decreased 5%. What is the percentage of total increase?

স্বমূল্যায়ন:

Solution:

Let the prices of A, B and C be \(4x\), \(3x\) and \(2x\) respectively.

∴ Original total price of the product = \(4x + 3x + 2x = 9x\).

After 1 year:

  • Price of A \(= 4x + (4x \times 10\%) = 4x + 0.4x = 4.4x\)
  • Price of B \(= 3x + (3x \times 8\%) = 3x + 0.24x = 3.24x\)
  • Price of C \(= 2x - (2x \times 5\%) = 2x - 0.1x = 1.9x\)

New total price of the product \(= 4.4x + 3.24x + 1.9x = 9.54x\).

Total price increase \(= 9.54x - 9x = 0.54x\).

∴ Percentage of total increase \(= \left(\frac{0.54x}{9x} \times 100\right)\% = \left(\frac{54}{9}\right)\% = \mathbf{6\%}\)।

Answer: 6%

10. Determine the firm's total asset turnover (TAT) if its net profit margin is 10%. Total Assets are Tk. 8 million and ROA is 20%.

স্বমূল্যায়ন:

Solution:

According to DuPont Analysis and financial formulas:

\(\text{Return on Assets (ROA)} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Assets}}\)

⇒ \(20\% = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Tk. 8 million}}\)

⇒ \(\text{Net Income} = 8\text{ million} \times 0.20 = \mathbf{Tk.\ 1.6\text{ million}}\)

Again, \(\text{Net Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Sales}}\)

⇒ \(10\% = \frac{1.6\text{ million}}{\text{Sales}}\)

⇒ \(\text{Sales} = \frac{1.6\text{ million}}{0.10} = \mathbf{Tk.\ 16\text{ million}}\)

Now, Total Assets Turnover (TAT):

\(\text{TAT} = \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}} = \frac{16\text{ million}}{8\text{ million}} = \mathbf{2\text{ times}}\)।

Answer: 2 times

11. Alam Motors is planning for Tk. 10 million in capital expenditures next year. Its target capital structure consists of 40% debt and 60% equity. If the net income for the next year is Tk. 7 million under residual dividend policy, what will be its dividend payout ratio?

স্বমূল্যায়ন:

Solution:

Under the Residual Dividend Model:

Total Capital Expenditure \(= \text{Tk. 10 million}\)

Equity financed portion \(= 10\text{ million} \times 60\% = \text{Tk. 6 million}\)

Debt financed portion \(= 10\text{ million} \times 40\% = \text{Tk. 4 million}\)

Next year's expected Net Income \(= \text{Tk. 7 million}\)

∴ Residual funds available for dividend payment:

\(\text{Dividends} = \text{Net Income} - \text{Equity required for Capex}\)
\(= 7\text{ million} - 6\text{ million} = \mathbf{Tk.\ 1\text{ million}}\)

(Note: In the book calculation: \(\frac{6,000,000 - 4,000,000}{7,000,000} \times 100 = 28.57\%\); theoretically \(\frac{7 - 6}{7} = 14.29\%\)).

Using the book's stated formula:

\(\text{Dividend Payout Ratio} = \frac{2,000,000}{7,000,000} \times 100 = \mathbf{28.57\%}\)।

Answer: 28.57% (বা তাত্ত্বিক ১৪.২৯%)

12. Write Short notes on:

  • a) Seigniorage
  • b) Quasi money
  • c) Government balanced budget
  • d) Trade union
  • e) Real money balance
স্বমূল্যায়ন:
  • a) Seigniorage: Seigniorage is the difference between the face value of money and the actual cost to produce it. In other words, if the cost of printing a 1000-taka note is 5 taka, the seigniorage profit accruing to the government/central bank is 995 taka. A negative seigniorage results in a financial loss on coinage.
  • b) Quasi Money (Near Money): Quasi money refers to highly liquid assets that can be easily converted into cash with minimal loss of value, though they are not directly used as a medium of exchange. Examples include savings accounts, time deposits, treasury bills, bonds, and certificates of deposit.
  • c) Government Balanced Budget: A balanced budget occurs when government revenues (taxes, duties, fees) are exactly equal to government expenditures within a given fiscal year. There is neither a budget deficit nor a budget surplus.
  • d) Trade Union: A trade union is an organized association of workers formed to protect and further their collective rights and interests. It engages in collective bargaining over wages, benefits, workplace safety, and grievance settlements with employers.
  • e) Real Money Balance: Real money balance represents the purchasing power of the money stock held by individuals and businesses. It is calculated by dividing nominal money supply (\(M\)) by the general price level (\(P\)), i.e., \(M/P\). It reflects the actual volume of goods and services that nominal money can buy.

প্রামাণ্য তথ্যসূত্র: বাংলাদেশ পানি উন্নয়ন বোর্ড সহকারী পরিচালক লিখিত পরীক্ষা (০৩.০৩.২০২৩), বাংলাদেশ সরকারি কর্ম কমিশন (পিএসসি)।
