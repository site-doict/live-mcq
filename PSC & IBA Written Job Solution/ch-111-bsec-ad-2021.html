প্রশ্ন নং ২ English English পূর্ণমান: ১০
Recent figures show an increase in violent crime among youngsters in Dhaka city. Some psychologists claim that the basic reason for this is that children these days are not getting the social and emotional learning they need from parents and teachers. To what extent do you agree or disagree with these statements? You should write approximately 200 words. Use your ideas, knowledge and experience and support your arguments with examples and with relevant evidence.
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Essay: Youth Violence and the Deficit in Social-Emotional Learning

Recent statistics showing an alarming escalation in juvenile delinquency and teenage gang violence—colloquially known as 'Kishore Gang' culture in Dhaka—have sparked deep societal concern. Prominent psychologists argue that this distressing trend stems primarily from an acute deficit in social and emotional learning (SEL) traditionally imparted by parents and educators. I strongly agree with this assertion, as character formation and emotional resilience are fundamentally rooted in domestic and school environments.

In modern urban Dhaka, hyper-competitive academic pressure and relentless parental work schedules have severely eroded meaningful familial communication. Many children are emotionally alienated, seeking validation, belonging, and identity in peer groups that often morph into aggressive street gangs. Furthermore, the modern schooling system prioritizes rote memorization and GPA benchmarks over moral values, empathy, conflict resolution, and civic responsibility. Deprived of emotional guidance, frustrated teenagers fall prey to substance abuse and weaponized violence.

However, while the lack of social and emotional nurturing is the bedrock cause, other compounding catalysts—such as the unchecked proliferation of violent digital media, cyberbullying, and local political patronization of delinquent youths—intensify the crisis. To reverse this menace, educational curricula must integrate formal socio-emotional learning, and parents must prioritize emotional presence over material indulgence. A compassionate, values-driven upbringing remains our strongest shield against youth criminality.

প্রশ্ন নং ৩ গণিত গণিত পূর্ণমান: ৬
Working alone at their respective constant rates, A can complete a task in 'a' days and B in 'b' days. They take turns in doing the task with each working 2 days at a time. If A starts they finish the task in exactly 10 days. If B starts, they take half a day more (10.5 days). How long does it take to complete the task if they both work together?
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Solution:

Daily work rate of A = \( \frac{1}{a} \), and daily work rate of B = \( \frac{1}{b} \).
They take turns working 2 days at a time.

Case 1: A starts first (Total 10 days):
- Days 1–2: A works (2 days)
- Days 3–4: B works (2 days)
- Days 5–6: A works (2 days)
- Days 7–8: B works (2 days)
- Days 9–10: A works (2 days)
In 10 days, A works for \( 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 \) days, and B works for \( 2 + 2 = 4 \) days.
Total work done = 1:
\[ \frac{6}{a} + \frac{4}{b} = 1 \quad \implies 6b + 4a = ab \quad \text{--- (i)} \]

Case 2: B starts first (Total 10.5 days = 10 1/2 days):
- Days 1–2: B works (2 days)
- Days 3–4: A works (2 days)
- Days 5–6: B works (2 days)
- Days 7–8: A works (2 days)
- Days 9–10: B works (2 days)
- Day 10.5 (remaining 0.5 day): A works (0.5 day)
In 10.5 days, B works for \( 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 \) days, and A works for \( 2 + 2 + 0.5 = 4.5 \) days.
Total work done = 1:
\[ \frac{4.5}{a} + \frac{6}{b} = 1 \quad \implies 4.5b + 6a = ab \quad \text{--- (ii)} \]

Equating (i) and (ii):
\[ 6b + 4a = 4.5b + 6a \]
\[ 6b - 4.5b = 6a - 4a \implies 1.5b = 2a \implies a = \frac{1.5}{2} b = \frac{3}{4} b \]

Substitute \( a = \frac{3}{4}b \) into (i):
\[ \frac{6}{3b/4} + \frac{4}{b} = 1 \implies \frac{24}{3b} + \frac{4}{b} = 1 \implies \frac{8}{b} + \frac{4}{b} = 1 \implies \frac{12}{b} = 1 \implies b = 12 \]
Then \( a = \frac{3}{4} \times 12 = 9 \) days.

Combined Work Together:
Rate of working together = \( \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{9} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{4 + 3}{36} = \frac{7}{36} \) of the work per day.
Time needed working together = \( \frac{36}{7} = 5\frac{1}{7} \) days.
Answer: \( \frac{36}{7} \) days (or \( 5\frac{1}{7} \) days).

প্রশ্ন নং ৪ গণিত গণিত পূর্ণমান: ৫
The cost of operating a toy company in the first year is $10,000 plus $2 for each toy. Assuming the company sells every toy it makes in the first year for $7, how many toys must the company sell to break even?
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Solution:

Fixed cost = $10,000
Variable cost per toy = $2
Selling price per toy = $7
Let \( x \) be the number of toys produced and sold to break even.
Total Cost \( TC = 10,000 + 2x \)
Total Revenue \( TR = 7x \)
At the break-even point, Total Revenue = Total Cost:
\[ 7x = 10,000 + 2x \]
\[ 5x = 10,000 \implies x = \frac{10,000}{5} = 2,000 \]
(Or using Contribution Margin: \( \text{BEP} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{P - V} = \frac{10,000}{7 - 2} = \frac{10,000}{5} = 2,000 \text{ toys} \)).
Answer: 2,000 toys.

প্রশ্ন নং ৫ গণিত গণিত পূর্ণমান: ৫
The posted weight limit for a wooden bridge in Bandarban is 6,000 kg. A delivery truck that is carrying x identical boxes each weighting 14 kg will pass over the bridge. If the combined weight of the empty delivery truck and its driver is 4,500 kg, what is the maximum possible value of x that will keep the combined weight of the truck, driver and boxes below the bridge's weight limit?
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Solution:

Maximum bridge weight limit = 6,000 kg.
Weight of empty truck and driver = 4,500 kg.
Weight of each box = 14 kg.
Total weight of \( x \) boxes = \( 14x \) kg.
Combined weight of truck, driver, and boxes = \( 4,500 + 14x \) kg.
To keep the combined weight strictly below (or within) the limit:
\[ 4,500 + 14x \le 6,000 \]
\[ 14x \le 6,000 - 4,500 \]
\[ 14x \le 1,500 \]
\[ x \le \frac{1,500}{14} \approx 107.1428 \]
Since \( x \) must be an integer number of whole boxes, the maximum possible value of \( x \) is 107.
Verification: \( 4,500 + (107 \times 14) = 4,500 + 1,498 = 5,998 \text{ kg} \le 6,000 \text{ kg} \). (108 boxes would weigh 6,012 kg, exceeding the limit).
Answer: 107 boxes.

What is cumulative preference share?
💡 সমাধান ও বিশ্লেষণ দেখতে ক্লিক করুন ▼

Cumulative Preference Share (সঞ্চয়ী অগ্রাধিকার শেয়ার):

A cumulative preference share is a class of preferred stock featuring a provision that any unpaid, passed, or skipped dividends automatically accumulate as arrears. If a company fails to declare or pay dividends in a financially difficult year, these dividend arrears accumulate on the balance sheet and must be paid in full to cumulative preferred shareholders before any dividends can be paid to common/ordinary shareholders in future profitable years.

Example: If a firm issues 10,000 cumulative preferred shares of Tk. 100 with an 8% dividend rate, annual dividend is Tk. 80,000. If unpaid for 2019 and 2020, in 2021 the company must pay Tk. 2,40,000 (Tk. 80,000 × 3 years) before declaring any dividend for equity shareholders.
