১. Pronoun-এর পারস্পরিক ক্রম (২৩১ বনাম ১২৩)

  • স্বাভাবিক বা শুভ কাজে (২৩১): 2nd person + 3rd person + 1st person বসে এবং verb সর্বদাই plural হয়।
    উদাহরণ: You, he and I are present. / You, he and I will take part.
  • দোষ স্বীকার বা অপরাধের ক্ষেত্রে (১২৩): 1st person + 2nd person + 3rd person বসে।
    উদাহরণ: I, you and he are guilty. / I, you and she were guilty.
  • বহুবচনের ক্ষেত্রে (১২৩): We, you and they সর্বদা 123 ক্রমানুসারে বসে।
    উদাহরণ: We, you and they will arrange the party.
  • 'Let'-এর পর Objective Form: Let-এর পর pronoun-এর objective রূপ (you and him) বসে: Let you and him be witnesses.

২. সাবজেক্ট-ভার্ব এগ্রিমেন্টের সোনালী নিয়ম

  • One of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb:
    One of my friends is a lawyer. (not friend/are).
  • Each / Every / Neither / Either:
    Each boy and each girl has a pen.
    Neither of the two sisters was present.
  • The number of বনাম A number of:
    ✦ The number of + plural noun + singular verb (The number of students is large).
    ✦ A number of + plural noun + plural verb (A number of boys are playing).
  • ভগ্নাংশ ও শতকরা (Fractions):
    ✦ Two-thirds of it is true. (singular pronoun/uncountable = singular verb).
    ✦ Two-thirds of the apples are rotten. (plural countable = plural verb).

৩. নেগেটিভ ইনভার্শন ও কনজাংশনের নিয়ম

  • No sooner had ... than:
    No sooner had I reached the station than the train left. (কখনোই when/then হবে না).
  • Scarcely / Hardly had ... when/before:
    Scarcely had he reached the school when the bell rang.
  • Lest ... should / might:
    Lest নিজে নেগেটিভ, তাই অপর অংশে no/not বসে না:
    Walk fast lest you should miss the train.
  • Though / Although:
    Though যুক্ত complex sentence-এ কমা (,) বসে, কিন্তু but বা yet বসে না:
    Though he is poor, he is honest.
  • Prefer ... to:
    I prefer reading to writing. / I prefer tea to coffee. (prefer-এর পর than বসে না).

৪. সাবজাংকটিভ ও কাল্পনিক শর্ত (Subjunctive & Conditionals)

  • It is high time / It is time:
    ✦ Subject থাকলে Past Indefinite verb: It is high time we changed our bad habits.
    ✦ Infinitive থাকলে: It is time to start the work.
  • Subjunctive Verbs (Insist / Suggest / Demand / Order):
    That-clause-এ subject-এর পর verb-এর base form বসে:
    He insisted that I go with him. / I suggested that he be present.
  • Wish / Fancy / As if / As though:
    অবাস্তব কল্পনা বা আকাঙ্ক্ষায় 'were' বা past tense বসে:
    I wish I were a king. / He talks as if he were mad.
