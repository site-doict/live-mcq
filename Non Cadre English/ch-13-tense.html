Part 1: প্রামাণ্য ইন্টারেক্টিভ রিভিশন নোট ও সূত্রাবলি

Exam Booster

📌 ১. Tense-এর মৌলিক পরিচয় ও ১২টি Tense-এর গঠন ম্যাট্রিক্স

Tense: ল্যাটিন শব্দ 'Tempus' (অর্থ Time বা সময়) থেকে Tense শব্দটির উৎপত্তি। Verb বা ক্রিয়ার কার্য সম্পাদনের সময় বা কালকে Tense বলে (Tense of a verb is mainly related to Time)।

Tense-এর নাম গঠন প্রণালি (Structure) বাস্তব উদাহরণ (Exam Models)
Present Indefinite Sub + V1 (s/es) + Ext. He drinks coffee. / She reads books.
Present Continuous Sub + am/is/are + V-ing + Ext. It is raining. / I am reading.
Present Perfect Sub + have/has + V3 + Ext. I have done my duty. / He has gone out.
Present Perfect Cont. Sub + have/has + been + V-ing + since/for + time It has been raining since morning.
Past Indefinite Sub + V2 + Ext. (did not + V1) Salam had a house. / He drank coffee.
Past Continuous Sub + was/were + V-ing + Ext. Boys were playing. / I was walking.
Past Perfect Sub + had + V3 + Ext. The train had started before we reached.
Past Perfect Cont. Sub + had + been + V-ing + since/for + time He had been waiting for three hours.
Future Indefinite Sub + shall/will + V1 + Ext. Ramiz will come again tomorrow.
Future Continuous Sub + shall/will + be + V-ing + Ext. He will be waiting for us.
Future Perfect Sub + shall/will + have + V3 + Ext. I shall have done the sum before he comes.
Future Perfect Cont. Sub + shall/will + have been + V-ing + time By 2026, I shall have been working here for 5 yrs.

⚡ ২. বিভ্রান্তিকর Strong & Weak Verbs (Past ও Past Participle রূপ)

অপরিবর্তিত Verbs (V1 = V2 = V3)
  • telecast → telecast → telecast
  • spread → spread → spread
  • split → split → split
  • put → put → put
  • cut, hit, let, read, set, shut, cost, shed, hurt (একই রূপ)।
পরীক্ষায় ঘনঘন আসা গুরুত্বপূর্ণ রূপান্তর
  • drink → drank → drunk
  • wear → wore → worn
  • swing → swung → swung
  • string → strung → strung
  • wring → wrung → wrung
  • seek → sought → sought
  • feed → fed → fed

⏳ ৩. 'Before' এবং 'After' এর অমোঘ নিয়মাবলি (Golden Rules)

💡 মনে রাখার জাদুকরী ট্রিকস:

Before মানে আগে → তাই Past Perfect বসবে Before-এর আগে!
After মানে পরে → তাই Past Perfect বসবে After-এর পরে!

কানেক্টর ১ম ক্লজ (First Clause) ২য় ক্লজ (Second Clause) পরীক্ষার প্রামাণ্য উদাহরণ
... before ... (Past) Past Perfect (had + V3) Past Indefinite (V2) The train had started before we reached the station. / His brother had left before I reached there.
... after ... (Past) Past Indefinite (V2) Past Perfect (had + V3) The teacher entered the class after the students had arrived.
... before ... (Future) Future Perfect (shall/will have + V3) Present Indefinite (V1) I shall have done the sum before the teacher comes. / I shall have finished the work before I leave.

🎯 ৪. Since বনাম For এবং Time Indicators

Since বনাম For (Perfect Continuous)

Since (Point of Time): নির্দিষ্ট শুরুর সময় বা সাল বোঝালে (যেমন: since morning, since 1988, since 1999, since 2012, since 7 o'clock, since last Monday, since Friday last)।

For (Period of Time): কাজের ব্যাপ্তি বা মোট সময়সীমা বোঝালে (যেমন: for three hours, for four days, for two years, for five hours, all day)।

Time Markers (Adverbial Indicators)

Past Indefinite নির্দেশক: yesterday, last night, last week, ago, long ago, in 1999, as soon as। (প্রশ্ন করতে: What time did you go to bed last night?)।

Present Perfect নির্দেশক: just, just now, already, recently, yet, ever, lately। (যেমন: He has gone out just now)।
