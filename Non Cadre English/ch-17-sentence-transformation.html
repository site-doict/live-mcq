📌 ১. বাক্যের প্রকারভেদ ও গঠন কাঠামো

  • Assertive (বিবৃতিমূলক): Sub + Verb + Obj/Ext। যেমন: "That's really stunning", "It is a beautiful bird"।
  • Imperative (আদেশ/উপদেশ/অনুরোধ): মূল Verb / Don't / Let দিয়ে শুরু। যেমন: "Always speak the truth", "Shut up!", "Go there at once", "Do call a taxi" (অনুরোধ), "Come what may" (Imperative Mood)।
  • Optative (প্রার্থনা/ইচ্ছা): May / Wish / Long দিয়ে শুরু। যেমন: "May God speed your recovery", "May he not suffer", "I wish you success in life"।
  • Exclamatory (বিস্ময়সূচক): What a / How + Adj/Noun + Sub + Verb + ! যেমন: "What a delicious meal!", "What a fool he is!" (Assertive: He is a great fool)।

📌 ২. Simple, Complex ও Compound বাক্য চেনার উপায়

  • Simple Sentence: কেবল ১টিমাত্র Subject ও ১টিমাত্র Finite Verb থাকে। যেমন: "Jumping up, he ran away", "In spite of being poor, he is honest"।
  • Complex Sentence: ১টি Principal Clause এবং ১ বা একাধিক Subordinate Clause থাকে। মার্কারে থাকে: who, which, that, what, when, where, if, unless, although, though, because, since, as, lest (should), so that। যেমন: "Walk fast lest you should miss the train", "If you read, you will learn"।
  • Compound Sentence: একাধিক Independent Clause যুক্ত থাকে and, but, or, yet, so, therefore, nor, either...or, neither...nor দ্বারা। যেমন: "Do or die", "Work or you will fail", "I want to lose weight, yet I eat chocolate daily"।

📌 ৩. বাক্য রূপান্তরের মাস্টার সূত্রাবলি

  • Too... to -> So... that: He is too weak to walk -> He is so weak that he cannot walk।
  • Must -> Cannot but: I must do it -> I cannot but do this / I cannot help doing this।
  • Affirmative to Negative: Auxiliary verb + not + বিপরীত শব্দ। Man is mortal -> No man is immortal।
  • Always -> Never: It always pours when it rains -> It never pours when it does not rain।
  • Nobody -> Everybody: Nobody likes a liar -> Everybody hates a liar।
  • Simple to Complex: A corrupt man cannot win respect -> A man who is corrupt cannot win the respect of others।

📌 ৫. ট্যাগ কোশ্চেনের গোল্ডেন রুলস

  • অর্ধ-নেগেটিভ শব্দ: বাক্যে scarcely, hardly, barely, seldom, rarely থাকলে ট্যাগ Affirmative হবে: "She could scarcely complain, could she?"
  • সর্বনাম They: Everybody, Everyone, Nobody, No one, Somebody থাকলে ট্যাগের Subject হবে they। যেমন: "Everybody hates a liar, don't they?"
  • I am এর ট্যাগ: I am going -> aren't I? (am not I? ব্যাকরণগতভাবে অশুদ্ধ)।
  • Imperative ট্যাগ: আদেশ/অনুরোধে will you? / won't you? (Open the door, will you?)।
  • Let's বনাম Let me: Let's / Let us (প্রস্তাব) হলে shall we?; কিন্তু Let me / Let him (অনুমতি) হলে will you? বসে।

📌 ৬. শর্তসূচক বাক্য (Conditional Sentences)

  • 1st Conditional: If + Present Indefinite, Sub + will/shall/can/may + V1। যেমন: "If you run in the rain, you will catch cold"।
  • 2nd Conditional: If + Past Indefinite, Sub + would/could/might + V1। Be-verb হিসেবে সর্বদা were বসে: "If I were you, I would do it", "If I had another pen, I could help you"।
  • 3rd Conditional: If + Past Perfect (had + V3), Sub + would have / could have / might have + V3। যেমন: "If he had had the money, he would have bought a fast car"।
  • Inversion (ইনভার্সন): Had + Sub + V3 ..., Sub + would have + V3। যেমন: "Had I seen him there, I would have talked to him", "Had I known her, I would have met her"।
