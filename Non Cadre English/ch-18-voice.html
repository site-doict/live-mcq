📌 ১. বাচ্য পরিবর্তনের ৩টি মৌলিক স্তম্ভ ও সর্বজনীন সূত্র

Active Voice ➔ Passive Voice সাধারণ সূত্র:
Active-এর Object (Subjective রূপ) + Tense ও Person অনুযায়ী Auxiliary Verb (Be-verb) + মূল Verb-এর Past Participle (V3) + Preposition (by/to/at/with) + Active-এর Subject (Objective রূপ)।
  • Subject ↔ Object রূপান্তর: I ➔ me, We ➔ us, He ➔ him, She ➔ her, They ➔ them, Who ➔ whom।
  • কর্তা উহ্য থাকা (Omission of Agent): Active voice-এ One, someone, no one, nobody, everyone, people ইত্যাদি অনির্দিষ্ট কর্তা থাকলে Passive-এ সাধারণত by + object বসে না। যেমন: "One should keep one's promises" ➔ "Promises should be kept."
  • প্যাসিভে সর্বদা V3: Passive voice-এর অন্যতম প্রধান বৈশিষ্ট্য হলো মূল ক্রিয়াটি সর্বদা Past Participle ($V_3$) রূপে বসে।

📌 ২. বিভিন্ন Tense অনুযায়ী Be-verb রূপান্তর ছক

Tense Active Verb রূপ Passive Be-verb রূপ ($+ V_3$) প্রামাণ্য উদাহরণ
Present Indefinite V1 / s, es am / is / are + V3 I know him ➔ He is known to me.
Past Indefinite V2 (did / wrote) was / were + V3 We called him a fool ➔ He was called a fool by us.
Future Indefinite shall / will + V1 shall be / will be + V3 Who will do the work? ➔ By whom will the work be done?
Continuous Tense am/is/are/was/were + V-ing am/is/are/was/were + being + V3 He is writing a letter ➔ A letter is being written by him.
Perfect Tense have / has / had + V3 have / has / had + been + V3 I have lost my wallet ➔ My wallet has been lost.

📌 ৩. Modal Auxiliary ও Modal Perfect বাচ্য রূপান্তর

  • সাধারণ Modal (can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must, ought to):
    Structure: Object ➔ Subject + Modal + be + V3 + by + Subject ➔ Object
    যেমন: "You must do the work" ➔ "The work must be done by you."
    "You should not scold the boy" ➔ "The boy should not be scolded by you."
  • Semi-modal 'going to' / 'have to':
    "They are going to lock the gate at 7 O'Clock" ➔ "The gate is going to be locked at 7 o'clock."
    "It has to be done by me" (Passive) ➔ "I have to do it." (Active)
  • Modal Perfect (should have, would have, ought to have):
    Structure: Modal + have been + V3
    যেমন: "You should have helped him" ➔ "He should have been helped by you."

📌 ৪. Interrogative বাক্যের বাচ্য পরিবর্তন কৌশল

  • Do / Does / Did দিয়ে প্রশ্ন:
    Do/Does ➔ Am/Is/Are + Sub + V3 + by + Obj + ?
    Did ➔ Was/Were + Sub + V3 + by + Obj + ?
    যেমন: "Do you see the flower?" ➔ "Is the flower seen by you?"
  • Who দিয়ে প্রশ্ন:
    By whom + Auxiliary verb + Subject + (be/being/been) + V3 + ?
    যেমন: "Who did this?" ➔ "By whom was this done?"
    "Who has broken this jug?" ➔ "By whom has this jug been broken?"
    "Who is calling me?" ➔ "By whom am I being called?"
    "Who can avoid death?" ➔ "By whom can death be avoided?"
  • Whom দিয়ে প্রশ্ন:
    Whom ➔ Who + Tense অনুযায়ী Be-verb + V3 + by + Obj + ?
    যেমন: "Whom do you want?" ➔ "Who is wanted by you?"
    "Whom did she accuse?" ➔ "Who was accused by her?"

📌 ৫. Imperative বাক্যের বাচ্য পরিবর্তন

  • মূল Verb দ্বারা শুরু (আদেশ/নির্দেশ):
    Let + Object + be + V3
    যেমন: "Do it" ➔ "Let it be done." | "Open the window" ➔ "Let the window be opened." | "Shoot the tiger" ➔ "Let the tiger be shot."
  • না-বোধক আদেশ (Don't / Do not):
    Noun Object: Let not + Noun Object + be + V3
    Pronoun Object: Let + Pronoun Object + not + be + V3
    যেমন: "Don't do it" ➔ "Let it not be done." (it সর্বনাম)
    "Do not shut the door" ➔ "Let not the door be shut." (door বিশেষ্য)
  • Never যুক্ত বাক্য:
    Let + Object + never + be + V3
    যেমন: "Never tell a lie" ➔ "Let a lie never be told."
  • Let + ব্যক্তিবাচক Object যুক্ত বাক্য:
    Let + Direct Object + be + V3 + by + ব্যক্তিবাচক Object
    যেমন: "Let her sing a song" ➔ "Let a song be sung by her."
    "Let me write a letter" ➔ "Let a letter be written by me."
  • অনুরোধ বুঝালে (Please / Kindly):
    You are requested to + Verb-এর base form + বাকি অংশ
    যেমন: "Please keep quiet" ➔ "You are requested to keep quiet."
    "Please close the door" ➔ "You are requested to close the door."

📌 ৬. ব্যতিক্রমী Preposition ও বিশেষ বাচ্য রূপান্তর

  • Preposition 'By'-এর ব্যতিক্রমী তালিকা:
    • Known + to: I know him ➔ He is known to me.
    • Surprised / Shocked + at: His comment surprised me ➔ I was surprised at his comment.
    • Pleased + with: Your behaviour pleased us ➔ We were pleased with your behaviour.
    • Filled / Seized + with: Smoke filled the room ➔ The room was filled with smoke. / Panic seized the writer ➔ The writer was seized with panic.
    • Annoyed + at (আচরণ) / with (ব্যক্তি): His conduct annoyed me ➔ I was annoyed at his conduct.
    • Informed + of: The police was informed of the matter.
  • Factitive Verb (Double Object): Call, make, elect, name, choose প্রভৃতি ক্রিয়াপদে ব্যক্তিবাচক কর্মটিই Subject হয়, Factitive পদটি অপরিবর্তিত থাকে।
    যেমন: "They elected him captain" ➔ "He was elected captain by them."
  • Bare Infinitive (See, hear, make, help): Active-এ to উহ্য থাকলেও Passive-এ to + V1 বসে।
    যেমন: "He heard her sing" ➔ "She was heard to sing by him."
    "You made him cook rice" ➔ "He was made to cook rice."
    "He helped me do it" ➔ "I was helped by him to do it."
  • Quasi-Passive Verb (কর্মহীন ভাববাচ্য): যেসকল Verb অর্থগতভাবে Passive কিন্তু গঠনে Active।
    Complement ছাড়া: "The house is building" ➔ "The house is being built."
    Complement সহ: "Honey tastes sweet" ➔ "Honey is sweet when it is tasted."
  • Group Verb / Prepositional Verb: Preposition টি মূল Verb-এর সাথেই যুক্ত থাকবে।
    যেমন: "She played on the flute" ➔ "The flute was played on by her."
    "The boy laughed at the lame man" ➔ "The lame man was laughed at by the boy."
    "My uncle looks after me" ➔ "I am looked after by my uncle."
