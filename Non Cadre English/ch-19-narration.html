১ম অংশ: প্রামাণ্য ইন্টারঅ্যাক্টিভ রিভিশন নোট ও সূত্রাবলি

(ব্যাখ্যা ও তত্ত্ব হতে সংকলিত)

📌 ১. উক্তি পরিবর্তনের মৌলিক কাঠামো ও Person পরিবর্তন

  • মৌলিক উপাদান: বক্তার নিজস্ব উদ্ধৃতি হলো Direct Speech (ইনভার্টেড কমার ভেতরের অংশ Reported Speech, বাইরের অংশ Reporting Verb) এবং অন্যের ভাষায় ব্যক্ত রূপ হলো Indirect Speech।
  • Person পরিবর্তন সূত্র:
    • 1st Person (I, we, my, us): Reporting verb-এর Subject অনুযায়ী পরিবর্তিত হয়।
    • 2nd Person (you, your): Reporting verb-এর Object অনুযায়ী পরিবর্তিত হয়।
    • 3rd Person (he, she, they, it): অপরিবর্তিত থাকে (No Change)।
  • নিকটবর্তী শব্দ থেকে দূরত্ববাচক রূপান্তর:
    this ➔ that | these ➔ those | now ➔ then | here ➔ there | today ➔ that day | tomorrow ➔ the next day / the following day | yesterday ➔ the previous day | come ➔ go।

📌 ২. Tense পরিবর্তনের সম্পূর্ণ রূপান্তর ছক (Reporting Verb Past হলে)

Direct Speech-এর Tense Indirect Speech-এর Tense প্রামাণ্য উদাহরণ
Present Indefinite (V1) Past Indefinite (V2) "I know this boy" ➔ said that he knew that boy.
Present Continuous Past Continuous "What are you doing?" ➔ asked what I was doing.
Present Perfect Past Perfect "I have waited an hour" ➔ said that he had waited an hour.
Past Indefinite (V2) Past Perfect (had + V3) "Why were you absent?" ➔ asked why I had been absent.
Future (Shall / Will) Would + V1 "I shall go home tomorrow" ➔ said that he would go home the next day.
Modal: Must Had to + V1 "I must write a letter" ➔ said that he had to write a letter.

📌 ৩. Tense অপরিবর্তিত থাকার সোনালী নিয়মাবলি (Exceptions)

  • Reporting Verb Present বা Present Perfect হলে:
    Reporting Verb যদি says, has said, will say হয়, তবে Reported Speech-এর Tense-এর কোনো পরিবর্তন হবে না।
    যেমন: Kalam says, "I did not go" ➔ Kalam says that he did not go.
    He has said to me, "I will go" ➔ He has told me that he will go.
  • চিরন্তন সত্য ও বৈজ্ঞানিক সত্য (Universal / Scientific Truth):
    Reporting Verb Past tense হলেও চিরন্তন সত্যের Tense কখনো বদলায় না:
    • The teacher said, "The earth moves round the sun." ➔ The teacher said that the earth moves round the sun.
    • The teacher said, "Honesty is the best policy." ➔ The teacher said that Honesty is the best policy.
    • He said, "Man is mortal." ➔ He said that man is mortal.

📌 ৪. Interrogative বাক্যের উক্তি রূপান্তর

  • Auxiliary Verb দ্বারা প্রশ্ন (Yes/No Question):
    Structure: Subject + asked / wanted to know + object + if / whether + Subject + Assertive Tense
    যেমন: She asked me, "Are you happy with your life?" ➔ She asked me if I was happy with my life.
    He said to me, "Do you like music?" ➔ He asked me if I liked music.
    I said to him, "Is he a Doctor?" ➔ I asked him whether he was a doctor.
  • Wh-Question (Who, Which, What, When, Where, Why, How):
    Structure: Subject + asked + object + Wh-word + Subject + Assertive Tense (if/whether বসে না)
    যেমন: "What are you doing?" ➔ She asked me what I was doing.
    "Why were you absent?" ➔ The teacher asked me why I had been absent.
    "What do you want?" ➔ He asked me what I wanted.

📌 ৫. Imperative বাক্যের উক্তি রূপান্তর ও Let-এর ব্যবহার

  • আদেশ, উপদেশ ও অনুরোধ:
    Subject + ordered / advised / requested / told + Object + to / not to + V1
    • উপদেশ: The teacher said to Alam, "Work regularly." ➔ The teacher advised Alam to work regularly.
    • নিষেধ: "Don't waste time" ➔ The teacher advised the students not to waste time.
    • অনুরোধ: Sally said, "Please get me a drink" ➔ Sally asked me to get her a drink.
    • আদেশ: She said to me, "Stand up" ➔ She told me to stand up.
  • Let's / Let us (প্রস্তাব):
    Subject + proposed to / suggested + that + we/they should + V1
    যেমন: My friend said to me, "Let's leave the place." ➔ My friend proposed to me that we should leave the place.
  • Let me / him / them (অনুমতি বা ইচ্ছা):
    Subject + requested / wished + that + subject + might / might be allowed to + V1
    যেমন: He said, "Let me have some milk." ➔ He wished that he might have some milk.
    She said, "Let me come in." ➔ She requested that she might come in.

📌 ৬. Exclamatory, শুভেচ্ছা ও বিশেষ উক্তি রূপান্তর

  • Exclamatory Sentence:
    Subject + exclaimed that + Subject + was/were + a very / great + Noun/Adj
    যেমন: He said, "What a cold day!" ➔ He exclaimed that it was a very cold day.
    He said, "What a pity!" ➔ He explained/exclaimed that it was a great pity.
  • ধন্যবাদ ও শুভেচ্ছা (Thank you & Good morning):
    • He said to me, "Thank you" ➔ He thanked me.
    • The teacher said, "Thank you, boys" ➔ The teacher thanked the boys.
    • He said, "Good morning Mr. Kamal" ➔ He wished Mr. Kamal good morning.
  • বিশেষ সম্বোধন (Sir, My Friend, You Liar):
    • "Sir" সম্বোধনে: Addressing me as sir, he respectfully exclaimed...
    • "My friend" সম্বোধনে: Addressing him as my friend, I told him to go in.
    • "You liar" আখ্যা দিলে: He called me a liar.
    • অভিশাপে: "Curse the flood" ➔ The old man cursed the flood.
