📌 ১. সাবজেক্ট-ভার্ব এগ্রিমেন্ট (Subject-Verb Agreement)

  • Neither/Either/Each/Every: বাক্যের শুরুতে Each, Every, Neither of, Either of থাকলে সর্বদা Singular Verb হয়। যেমন: Neither of the two boys was present.
  • Fraction / Percentage: ভগ্নাংশ বা পার্সেন্টেজ এর পরে singular/uncountable noun থাকলে singular verb, plural noun থাকলে plural verb। যেমন: Two-thirds of the work is done; Two-thirds of the students are present.
  • Distance / Money / Time: সামগ্রিক পরিমাণ বা দূরত্ব বোঝাতে Plural Noun হলেও Verb Singular হয়। যেমন: Ten miles is a long distance. Fifty thousand dollars was spent.
Rule: Indefinite Pronoun (Everyone/Nobody/Each) ➔ Singular Verb + Singular Possessive (his/its)

📌 ২. কন্ডিশনাল সেন্টেন্স ও লিঙ্কাস (Conditionals)

  • 1st Conditional: If + Present Simple ➔ Future Simple (will + V1)। যেমন: If it rains, we will stay home.
  • 2nd Conditional: If + Past Simple ➔ would/could/might + V1। যেমন: If I had money, I would buy a car. (Be verb হলে সর্বদা 'were')।
  • 3rd Conditional: If + Past Perfect ➔ would have + V3। যেমন: If you had studied, you would have passed.
  • Had inversion: Had I seen him, I would have given him the news.
Structure: Had + Sub + V3 ..., Sub + would have + V3

📌 ৩. সাবজাঙ্কটিভ মুড ও পজেসিভ ব্যবহার

  • Subjunctive Verbs: recommend, insist, suggest, demand, require, propose ইত্যাদির পর that-clause এ সর্বদা verb-এর Base Form (বা be) বসে; s/es বা modal বসে না। যেমন: I recommend that he be present.
  • Gerund-এর পূর্বে Possessive: Gerund (V-ing)-এর ঠিক পূর্বে Noun/Pronoun-এর possessive রূপ (my, his, her, their, its) বসে। যেমন: My father approved of her marrying.
  • Causative Verbs: Make/Let/Have + Person ➔ Bare Infinitive (V1)। যেমন: He made the thief run away. Let him go.

📌 ৪. ডিটারমিনার ও কোয়ান্টিফায়ার (Few / Little)

প্রকার Countable Noun Uncountable Noun
নেই বললেই চলে (Negative) few little
কিছুটা আছে (Positive) a few a little
নির্দিষ্ট অল্প সবটুকু the few the little
তুলনামূলক রূপ fewer less
💡 মনে রাখুন: People, cows, students = Countable (many, few)। আর Milk, tea, money, traffic, information, advice, furniture = Uncountable (much, little, less)।
